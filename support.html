Common questions

Why don't I see any activities?

Try widening your search radius or enabling more activity types in Settings. Coverage varies by location and sport, since listings come from mapping data rather than a paid business directory.

Why is a business's info incomplete?

Listings are combined from several mapping sources, so ratings, pricing and contact details aren't available for every spot.

Can I add a spot that's missing?

Yes — tap the + button on the Discover screen, or "Add This Spot" from the empty state, to save a location at your current position.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

Adrenaline Junkie Premium is billed through your Apple ID. Open the Settings app on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, to change or cancel it. You can also reach the subscription screen from Settings inside the app.
