Adrenaline Junkie

Privacy Policy

Last updated September 2026

Adrenaline Junkie is published by Eurus Production LLC. The app has no user accounts and no sign-up. This policy explains what leaves your device and why.

Location

With your permission, the app uses your device's location while the app is in use, to search for activities within your chosen radius. Your approximate coordinates are sent to the search providers listed below in order to run that search. Location access is optional — you can decline it and choose a search location manually instead.

We do not store a history of where you have been.

Search provider

Searches are sent to a service we operate, which passes them on to Google Places and returns the results. That service keeps a cache of results by approximate area — rounded to roughly a one-kilometre grid — so that repeated searches of the same area don't have to be re-run. The cache stores places, not people: it contains no device identifier, no account, and nothing that identifies who performed a search.

Our service records the standard request information a web server receives, including IP address, in order to apply rate limits and prevent abuse. These records are short-lived and are not used to build a profile of you.

Other data sources

The app also queries Apple Maps (MapKit) and OpenStreetMap's Overpass API for listings. Those requests are handled under Apple's and the OpenStreetMap Foundation's own privacy policies.

Stored on your device

Your favourites, cached search results, settings, free-view count, and any spots you add yourself are stored only on your device. They are not transmitted to us.

Subscriptions

Adrenaline Junkie Premium is an auto-renewing subscription processed by Apple through your Apple ID. Payment is handled entirely by Apple — we never see your payment details. We receive only Apple's confirmation of whether a subscription is active.

Advertising

The app shows ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers, including the IDFA if you grant tracking permission, to serve and measure ads. If you decline tracking, the app still works fully. This data is handled under Google's privacy policy.

Children

Adrenaline Junkie is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Your rights

Because the app has no accounts, we hold no personal profile to export or delete. To remove everything the app has stored about you, delete the app from your device. For any question about this policy, contact us at the address below.
