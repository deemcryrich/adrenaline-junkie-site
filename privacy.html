Privacy Policy

Last updated August 2026

Sidequest does not have user accounts and does not operate a backend server. Your data stays on your device except where noted below.

Location

The app uses your device's location, only while the app is in use, to search for nearby activities within your chosen radius. Your approximate coordinates are sent to OpenStreetMap's Overpass API to perform that search. We don't store your location on any server we operate.

Locally stored data

Your favorites, cached search results, settings, and any spots you add yourself are stored only on your device and are not transmitted to us.

Advertising

This app shows ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the IDFA, if you grant tracking permission) to serve and measure ads. This data is handled under Google's own privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to deem.cry.rich@cloak.id.
