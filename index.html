Eurus Production LLC

Independent iOS software studio

Eurus Production LLC designs, builds and publishes consumer applications for iPhone. We're a small independent studio based in Austin, Texas, working on apps that help people find things worth doing in the real world.

What we do

We build and operate our own apps end to end — design, engineering, backend infrastructure and ongoing support. We don't take client work or run advertising services for other companies.

  • Product design and development for iOS, built natively in Swift and SwiftUI.
  • Backend services that support our apps, including location search infrastructure and data caching.
  • Ongoing maintenance and user support for everything we publish on the App Store.

Our apps

Adrenaline Junkie

A location-based discovery app for extreme sports and outdoor adventure. It maps skydiving drop zones, dive sites, climbing gyms, whitewater rafting outfitters, off-road parks, helicopter tours and more than a dozen other activity types around wherever you happen to be.

Listings are aggregated from several mapping sources into one searchable, filterable feed, with the certification or licensing each activity actually requires shown alongside it. Available on iPhone.
