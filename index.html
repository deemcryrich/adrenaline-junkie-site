Common questions

Why don't I see any activities?

Try widening your search radius or enabling more activity types in Settings. Activity data comes from OpenStreetMap, so coverage varies by location and sport.

Why is a business's info incomplete?

Sidequest sources listings from OpenStreetMap's free, community-mapped data rather than a commercial business directory, so ratings, pricing, and contact info aren't always available.

Can I add a spot that's missing?

Yes — tap the + button on the Discover screen, or "Add This Spot" from the empty state, to save a location at your current position.
